# CRAlign — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is CRAlign?**
An AI-assisted musculoskeletal (MSK) wellness assessment platform with a licensed-clinician review workflow at its core.

**Does CRAlign replace a doctor or physical therapist?**
No. CRAlign is assistive. Every assessment goes through a licensed clinician review workflow — a qualified human always stays in the loop.

## How It Works

**What does "AI-assisted" mean?**
The AI helps with conversational intake and with structuring and surfacing information for the clinician. It does not make clinical decisions.

**What is the clinician review workflow?**
A structured step where a licensed clinician reviews each assessment before it is finalized, including routing to appropriately licensed professionals.

**What is state-based assignment?**
Cases are routed to clinicians licensed appropriately for the patient's location.

## Privacy & Compliance

**Is it built for healthcare data privacy?**
Yes. CRAlign uses a compliance-minded, privacy-first architecture.

## Product

**What stage is CRAlign at?**
CRAlign is currently at MVP stage.

**Who is it for?**
Rehabilitation clinics, physical therapy practices, telehealth providers, and clinician networks.
